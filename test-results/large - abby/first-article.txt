
The Hon. Alexander Mackenzie.

(From the Kingston News, Tory.)

The history of Great Britain and her colo-
nies has afforded many illustrations of men
rising from the lower ranks of life by means
of steady perseverance and unceasing mental
activity to places of the highest honor and
greatest responsibility in the State. It has
often been argued against the British monar-
chical system of Government that it is an
almost impassable barrier to those whose
ability and aspirations impel them upwards ;
but the facts prove the contrary. Men of
humble birth have risen to occupy high posi-
tions and have lived to be honored by the
nation at large. These thoughts suggest
themselves by the departure of the Premier of
the Dominion on an official visit to England.
